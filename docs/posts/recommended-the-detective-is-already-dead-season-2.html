Who this is for

This is for anyone who loves high-concept light novel mysteries mixed with wild urban fantasy. If you enjoyed the emotional rollercoaster of the first season, this continuation is made for you. It hits the spot for fans of stylish detective duos, tragic romance, secret organizations, and sci-fi twists. If you like anime that constantly shifts genres while keeping its character bonds at the center, grab your headphones and jump in.

When the original light novels by Nigojuu took off here in Japan, everyone fell in love with Siesta immediately. Studio ENGI brought her iconic white hair and sharp wit to screen, creating a total pop-culture moment. Even though the story starts with a massive tragedy, the mystery only gets bigger from there.

Season 2 picks up the pieces of Kimihiko Kimizuka’s chaotic life as he continues to live in the long shadow of his dead partner. It still matters today because it dares to mash together hard-boiled detective drama, secret sci-fi organizations, and touching romance. It proves that a great partnership never really ends, even after death.

Spoiler-light synopsis

Kimihiko Kimizuka thought his crazy adventure days were over after losing the legendary detective Siesta. He was ready to settle back into a boring high school life. But fate had completely different plans. Her legacy lives on through the people she touched, including Nagisa Natsunagi, a girl connected to Siesta in ways no one expected.

As new threats emerge from the shadows of the secret organization SPES, Kimihiko is dragged back into the world of high-stakes mysteries. Armed with inherited wills, hidden clues, and unresolved feelings, he has to navigate dangerous battlefields and psychological puzzles. The main question remains: can a legendary detective really solve cases from beyond the grave? Get ready for another wild ride across the globe where nothing is quite as simple as standard detective work.

Highlights and watch points

The Legacy of Siesta Lives On

Even when she isn't physically in the room, Siesta's presence dominates every scene. The show handles her memory with absolute respect while letting the surviving cast grow. Watching Kimihiko piece together her past plans feels like watching a grand chess game played across time. Her charm, mysterious smiles, and brilliant intellect still drive the entire plot forward, making every revelation about her past feel incredibly rewarding for longtime fans.

Genre-Bending Thrills and Urban Sci-Fi

Don't expect a basic courtroom mystery here. The series constantly swings between quiet dialogue-heavy clues and jaw-dropping action sequences. You get high-tech gadgets, superhuman villains, deep-seated conspiracies, and emotional romance all packed into one story. ENGI keeps the visual presentation slick, using dynamic camera angles and vibrant color palettes that make the urban environments pop right off your screen during intense showdowns.

Deepening Character Chemistry

The dynamic between Kimihiko and the surrounding cast reaches new emotional depth this season. The banter remains razor-sharp, but the shared grief and determination create a genuine sense of camaraderie. Watching Nagisa, Charlotte, and Kimihiko lean on each other while carving out their own paths creates the heart of the show. You root for them not just to solve the mystery, but to find peace with their own messy lives.
