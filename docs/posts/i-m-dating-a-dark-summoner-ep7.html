1. The Hook: What Made This Episode Unmissable

That sudden, sharp gasp right when the door handle rattles? Pure comedy gold. By episode 7, the sheer absurdity of Roni and Amona trying to keep their hands off each other while hiding five inches from the rest of the party hits critical mass. You can practically feel the room heating up through the screen. One second Roni is trying to cling to his righteous, uptight priest persona, and the next, Amona drops that wicked, half-lidded smirk that completely annihilates his self-control.

What makes this stretch of the season click so hard is how the comedy doesn't deflate the physical tension—it winds the spring tighter. You sit there in the dark during the broadcast, gripping your desk because you genuinely do not know if they are going to get caught red-handed by Eury and Haru or wreck the furniture first. It is messy, hilarious, and unapologetically fun.

2. Under the Hood: Direction & Craft That Stole the Show

  • Craft point: Director Seiya Miyajima keeps the timing surgical. The cutaways between Roni’s hyper-detailed, blood-drained panic face and Amona’s relaxed, predatory posture land so well because the edit holds on total silence for a split second before hitting the punchline sound cue.
  • Craft point: Chief Animation Director Takahiro Sasaki delivers some wonderful micro-expressions on Amona. Watch the twitch of her eyebrow right before she leans in to tease Roni; that split-second drawing shift sells both her wicked playfulness and the genuine affection lurking behind it.
  • Craft point: Sound Director Fumihiko Ootera pulls the audio carpet out from under you. Cutting the background music entirely during the near-miss moments leaves only heavy, suppressed breathing and cloth rustling, cranking the second-hand embarrassment to maximum volume.
  • Seiyuu craft: Rei Takano plays Amona with an intoxicating purr that slips effortlessly into flustered squeaks, but Yuuki Nishiyama owns this episode by volleying Roni from a solemn, resonant holy tone straight into a strangled falsetto panic, with Senmai Houdentei's Imp gleefully cackling on the sidelines like the ultimate chaos engine.

3. Domestic Radar: What Japanese Fans Are Screaming About

  • 「もう付き合っちゃえよ！」 (Just date already!): The universal late-night cry across 5ch and X threads whenever Roni and Amona behave like an old married couple while still frantically claiming this is just a meaningless, sinful arrangement.
  • 「インプ有能すぎる」 (Imp is way too competent / MVP): The typical line lighting up episode reaction roundups whenever the Imp acts as the ultimate instigator, effortlessly sabotaging Roni's excuses and shoving both leads back into the same compromised spot.
