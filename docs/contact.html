Contact Us

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Use this desk if you are a reader with a correction, a journalist, a licensed platform, or a rights holder. We read editorial and legal mail. We do not offer piracy help, leak trading, or unofficial download links.

Readers and press

Tell us the article URL, the episode number, and what needs a second look. Useful mail includes factual errors in metadata (studio, episode count, platform) and good-faith disagreements about craft that cite the episode rather than insults.

Editorial email: editorial@example.com

Rights holders and copyright agents

If you represent a production committee, publisher, licensor, or performer and believe a page exceeds legitimate criticism or uses an asset you want removed, send a notice that includes:

  • your name, role, and a contact email;
  • the work and the specific URL(s);
  • a description of the material (for example a key visual you want taken down);
  • a statement that you are authorized to act for the owner;
  • a good-faith signature (typed name is acceptable in email).

We aim to review valid notices promptly. Our default is to preserve criticism and to remove or replace assets that are not needed for commentary. We will not keep unauthorized episode video; this site does not host video files.
